Lesson 1: What Social Media Management Really Involves

1. Introduction

Ask ten people what a social media manager does, and most will say "posts on Instagram." That answer is about 10% correct. Behind every polished brand post is a person (or team) making strategic decisions about audience, timing, messaging, budget, and business goals — long before anything gets published.

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:


Define social media management and distinguish it from social media marketing.
Identify the core responsibilities a social media manager handles day-to-day, weekly, and monthly.
Understand where social media management fits inside a broader marketing team or business.


We'll start by defining the role clearly, then break down what the job actually looks like in practice.

2. Core Concepts & Explanations

What is Social Media Management?

Social media management is the ongoing process of creating, scheduling, analyzing, and engaging with content posted on social media platforms on behalf of a brand or individual, with the goal of building an audience, strengthening brand reputation, and driving measurable business results.

It's easy to confuse this with social media marketing, but they're related, not identical:

3. Practical Application / How-To

Pick a local business you're familiar with (a restaurant, a boutique, a gym). Sketch out what a realistic week of social media management would look like for them:


What would you post on Monday vs. Friday, and why?
How much time would go to creating content vs. responding to comments/DMs?
What's one thing you'd check weekly to know if it's working?


You don't need social media experience to do this — the goal is to start thinking like a social media manager, not just a social media user.
