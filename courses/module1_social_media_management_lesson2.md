Lesson 2: Understanding the Platform Landscape

1. Introduction

Not every brand belongs on every platform. A B2B software company doesn't need to be on TikTok just because TikTok is popular — and a skincare brand ignoring Instagram and TikTok entirely would be leaving huge reach on the table. Choosing the right platforms is one of the first strategic decisions a social media manager makes.

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:


Identify the major social media platforms and their primary audience.
Understand the content format each platform rewards.
Make an informed recommendation on which platforms a given business should prioritize.

How to Choose the Right Platforms

Rather than trying to be everywhere, a social media manager evaluates three questions for each platform:


Is my target audience actually here? (A retirement planning service has little reason to prioritize TikTok.)
Can I realistically produce the content format this platform rewards? (A platform that lives on video isn't a fit if you have zero video production capacity.)
Does this platform support my actual business goal? (LinkedIn suits B2B lead generation; Pinterest suits product discovery for physical goods.)


It's almost always better to run 2-3 platforms well than 6 platforms poorly.
