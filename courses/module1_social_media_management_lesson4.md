Lesson 4: Building a Social Media Strategy & Setting SMART Goals/KPIs

1. Introduction

"Grow our social media" is not a strategy — it's a wish. Without a clear goal and a way to measure progress, it's impossible to know whether your content is actually working or just keeping you busy. This lesson closes out Module 1 by turning everything you've learned so far into a real, measurable plan.

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:


Explain what a social media strategy document typically includes.
Apply the SMART framework to write clear, measurable social media goals.
Match common business goals to the right KPIs, avoiding vanity metrics.


2. Core Concepts & Explanations

What Goes Into a Social Media Strategy?

A social media strategy connects everything covered so far into one working document:


Business goal — what the business actually needs (more leads, more sales, stronger brand awareness)
Target platforms — from Lesson 2
Audience & persona — from Lesson 3
Content pillars — the 3-5 recurring themes content will rotate through
Goals & KPIs — how success will be measured
Posting cadence — how often, and on which platforms

Full SMART goal example: "Grow Instagram followers among Lagos-based small business owners by 1,000 within the next 90 days, through consistent Reels and community engagement."

Matching Goals to the Right KPIs

A common beginner mistake is tracking metrics that feel good but don't actually reflect business impact — these are often called vanity metrics (e.g., total follower count in isolation, without context).

Business GoalPrimary KPI(s)WhyBrand awarenessReach, impressions, follower growthMeasures how many new people are seeing the brandEngagement/communityEngagement rate, comments, sharesMeasures how actively the audience is interacting, not just watchingLead generationClick-through rate, website clicks, sign-upsMeasures whether content is actually driving action off-platformSalesConversion rate, revenue from social, ROAS (if running ads)Ties directly to business revenue

The right KPI always depends on the goal — there's no single metric that matters for every campaign.

3. Practical Application / How-To

For your Lesson 1-3 business, write one complete SMART goal, then pick the 1-2 KPIs you'd track weekly to know if you're on pace to hit it.
